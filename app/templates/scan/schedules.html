About Scheduled Scans

Scheduled scans allow you to automate the scanning process at regular intervals.

Available scheduling options:

  • Daily: Runs every day at the specified time
  • Weekly: Runs on a specific day of the week
  • Monthly: Runs on a specific day of the month

Schedules can target an entire environment or a specific subnet within an environment.
